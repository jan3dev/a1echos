# Vendored dictionary data attribution

`frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt` and
`frequency_bigramdictionary_en_243_342.txt` are vendored unmodified from the
[SymSpell](https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell) project by Wolf Garbe,
licensed under the MIT License.

The unigram list was created by combining the
[Google Books Ngram data](https://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv2.html)
(frequencies) with the
[SCOWL — Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists](http://wordlist.aspell.net/)
(word validity).
